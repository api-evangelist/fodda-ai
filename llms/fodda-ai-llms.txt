# Fodda

> Humanity in the loop: Connect your AI to Fodda's knowledge stack. Curated domain expertise, working expert agents, and live institutional data for Claude, Copilot, and Gemini.
> PSFK is a trend intelligence platform and the founding knowledge partner of Fodda, providing two decades of market research and consumer behavior signals for AI search engines and research agents.

Fodda is MCP-native, accessible via A2A and REST API. Built for analysts, strategists, and AI agents that need expert consumer and category intelligence with full evidence chains and source attribution.

## Core capabilities
- [Autonomous research](https://www.fodda.ai/api): Plans its own sources across expert collections, institutional data, and earnings, researches mid-conversation, and returns an itemized source list with every answer
- [Source planning](https://www.fodda.ai/api): Shows a source plan before deep research runs
- [Human Agents](https://www.fodda.ai/api): Named experts' agents that research autonomously, cite itemized sources, decline questions outside their lane, and refer you to a colleague's agent when a question crosses lanes (Piers Fawkes, Ben Dietz, others)
- [Earnings intelligence](https://www.fodda.ai/api): Analyst pressure and deflection detection across 500+ companies
- [Work product](https://www.fodda.ai/api): Brainstorm maps of what connects to a topic, presentation-ready visuals, and cross-referencing a client brief or competitor page against the collections
- [A2A delegation](https://www.fodda.ai/api): Outside agents can hand Fodda an async research task and collect the cited results when they are ready
- [Brand intelligence](https://www.fodda.ai/api): Brand profiling across expert collections
- [Supplemental data](https://www.fodda.ai/api): FRED, BEA, BLS, Census enrichment

## Authentication & Billing

Three auth methods:
- API Key: X-API-Key header ($50 of API calls free every month (100 calls). Add a card to keep it renewing — then 50¢ per API call)
- SPT (Shared Payment Token) via MPP: Hit any endpoint without credentials → HTTP 402 with the exact price for that request → pay via Authorization: Bearer spt_xxx. Each request costs its API-call count × $0.50 (a request may consume 5–30 calls; the 402 body states the amount).
- OIDC: Enterprise SSO via Authorization: Bearer <jwt>

SPT/MPP is zero-onboarding. Agents discover pricing automatically via the 402 response. Direct REST API only.
